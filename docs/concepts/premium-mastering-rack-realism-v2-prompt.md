## Revised rack concept

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: revised desktop design concept for the Project Sequencer Premium
mastering rack

Primary request: preserve the exact overall app layout, serial rack structure,
input/output patch lane, equipment order, and readable control hierarchy from
the v1 reference, while making the analog equipment materially more
photorealistic and convincingly aged.

Materials and details: champagne brushed aluminum with rubbed edges for EQ;
deep navy hammertone enamel for compressor; charcoal powder-coat for output;
aged silver aluminum for limiter; subtle fingerprints, hairline scratches,
paint chips only at edges and screw holes, oxidized rack screws, recessed seams,
engraved legends, warm backlit meter glass, metal toggle guards, and black
Bakelite knobs with knurled edges and realistic indicator inserts.

Branding: original fictional manufacturer marks such as Harbor Signal,
Northline Audio, and Ironvale Labs. No real brand, logo, product, or trade
dress.

Constraints: retain every processor, control, navigation item, meter, and
workflow; preserve readable contrast; keep grunge restrained and believable;
no real logos, trademarks, watermarks, people, or unrelated objects.
